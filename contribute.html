Ways to contribute

Contributions can be as small as an arithmetic correction or as ambitious as a new executable laboratory. We especially value contributions that increase clarity and mathematical correctness.

  1. Report an error. Include the book title, edition, page number, the erroneous statement, and what you believe is correct.
  2. Improve an explanation. Offer a smaller example, sharper diagram, or a proof that makes the key idea unavoidable.
  3. Add an exercise. Supply a precise problem and a checked solution or substantial hint.
  4. Develop code. In a companion repository, add reproducible demonstrations, tests, and short usage documentation.
  5. Suggest a new volume. Propose a hard topic that can be illuminated with a few well-chosen mathematical structures.
Important distinction

This repository is the central library and reader. The companion project repositories are the best place for code contributions specific to an individual book. Book manuscript corrections may require review and incorporation into a future PDF edition by the authors; changing the hosted PDF alone is not an appropriate substitute for revising the source.

A good issue report

Use a title such as [Information Geometry v0.2, p. 13] Index mismatch in Eq. 4. Then provide the smallest change that makes the mathematics correct. A small concrete counterexample or reproducible computation is particularly helpful.

Pull requests

For a website improvement, fork the library repository, create a branch, and open a pull request. To add a new booklet, introduce the PDF in books/, a cover preview in assets/covers/, and its catalogue metadata in assets/js/books.js. Check that the in-browser reader and download button still work.

See the repository's full contribution guidelines for filenames, review expectations, and edition/version rules.

Authorship and permissions

Book copyrights remain with their respective authors, including coauthors. The presence of a PDF on this site does not grant a blanket permission to republish it, sell it, or produce derivative editions. Any future open license for the book texts should be explicitly approved by the relevant authors. By submitting a suggestion or a pull request, you agree that the project maintainers may review it; incorporation into manuscripts is subject to explicit authorization and appropriate attribution.

Get started

Choose a volume from the bookshelf, read its first few pages, and tell us what could be made clearer. That is already a meaningful contribution.
